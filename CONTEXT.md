# JR Health & Fitness

An online personal-training business run by one coach in the UK, mainly helping people lose weight. This repo is its public landing page, which turns visitors into Enquiries.

## Language

### The business

**JR Health & Fitness**:
The business's one official name, used everywhere it is named.
_Avoid_: JR Fitness & Health Academy, JR Fitness, Joe's website

**Coach**:
Joe Russell, the one person who delivers the Program and replies to Enquiries.
_Avoid_: Trainer, PT, instructor

### The offer

**Program**:
The 6-Week Program: the one coaching package on sale. It lasts at least 6 weeks and then rolls on monthly.
_Avoid_: Plan, premium plan, package, subscription

**Inclusion**:
One of the five things a Client gets in the Program: a tailored workout plan, a tracking app, budget meal sheets, a weekly call with the Coach, and a habits guide.
_Avoid_: Feature, perk, benefit

### People

**Prospect**:
A visitor who has sent an Enquiry but has not started the Program.
_Avoid_: Lead, sign-up, applicant

**Client**:
A person who is currently on the Program, or has been.
_Avoid_: Customer, member, subject, trial subject

**Enquiry**:
A message a visitor sends through the landing page asking to hear more about the Program. It is the page's one main action. It is not a booking and not a payment.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, application, registration, contact request

**Success Story**:
One Client's published result: before-and-after photos, a first name, the outcome and a short quote. It is only shown with that Client's written permission.
_Avoid_: Trial subject, transformation, review
