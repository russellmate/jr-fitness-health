Questions

How long is the Program?

Six weeks to start, for £300. After that you can carry on for £60 a month and cancel anytime.

I've never really trained. Is this for me?

Yes. Your plan is written for your level, and Joe adjusts it every week. Plenty of people start from nothing.

Will it be hard?

Honestly, the first couple of weeks take willpower. It gets easier the further in you get, because the plan turns into habit.

Do I have to follow a strict diet?

No crash diets. You get budget meal sheets with quick, easy meals, and you log what you eat in the app so Joe can keep it matched to your goal.

What happens on the weekly call?

You and Joe go through your week: progress, what's working, what's a struggle, and any questions. Joe changes your plan if something needs to.
